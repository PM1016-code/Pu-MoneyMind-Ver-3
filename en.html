Is this for you?

Know whether this program fits you

This may suit you if…

  • You are a beginner who wants to start with solid foundations and a risk framework
  • You have traded before but still enter and exit emotionally or without a plan
  • You are ready to learn, practise, collect data and review your decisions
  • You want a Community and Coaching to help identify areas for improvement

This may not suit you if…

  • You want ready-made signals without learning
  • You expect guaranteed profit, shortcuts or rapid results
  • You are not prepared to accept financial-market risk
  • You want to increase trade size to accelerate results without a plan
