Professional trading tools

Helpful tools that can make trade planning clearer

GSS and TTS can help you read context and trends step by step, but you still need to check the risk and make the final call yourself.

Gold Swing Strategy

Ideal for swing traders who wait for larger moves and do not want to watch charts all day.Helps you read the market step by step—from price structure and key zones to market context—so you can see when to wait, which zones deserve attention and which setups may be worth planning for a swing trade.

Trend Tunnel Strategy

Ideal for trend-following traders who work with momentum and look to move with the market.Helps filter direction, trend and momentum so you can see where the market may be heading, whether momentum is holding or weakening and where to wait for a possible trend-following setup.
